About this app

Final year project (Grade A), BS Hons Software Engineering, Virtual University of Pakistan · Rizwan Ali

HeartCare estimates a patient's heart disease risk from 13 clinical measurements. Three models trained on 597 patient records each give a probability; the app averages them and reports a risk level: Low below 30%, Medium from 30% to 70%, and High at 70% or above.

Models

  • Logistic Regression and Random Forest (scikit-learn)
  • Neural network (TensorFlow/Keras): Dense 16 → Dropout 0.3 → Dense 8 → Dense 1
  • In repeated stratified 5-fold cross-validation, logistic regression and the random forest reached ROC-AUC 0.90 (accuracy 0.82–0.83). Recall stayed below precision, so some at-risk patients would be missed.

This web version

  • Uses the same trained models as the Flask app, exported from the project's model files. On 598 test inputs it gives exactly the same probabilities and risk levels as the Python app.
  • The original app adds a login and stores history in MySQL. Here, history is saved only in your browser.
